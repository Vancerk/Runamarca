# Retratos conceituais dos Patronos

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`. São referências visuais originais para os três Patronos de teste, substituíveis pelas artes escolhidas para o cenário. Arquivos: `garra-vigilante.png`, `olho-dos-pactos.png`, `bigorna-desperta.png`.

## A Garra Vigilante

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: character portrait art for the Patron card 'A Garra Vigilante' in a dark fantasy western bounty-hunting card game. Primary request: a living human frontier bounty hunter, weathered face, watchful expression, layered leather coat and practical scarf, a restrained claw-shaped metal badge, carrying a holstered flintlock-inspired firearm; clearly a character, not an abstract deity or magic symbol. Style: painterly fantasy card illustration with hand-inked details, aged parchment warmth and subtle worn stone texture, coherent with an ochre and terracotta card back. Composition: vertical portrait, torso and face centered, enough negative space at edges for UI cropping. Lighting: warm late-afternoon contrast, dramatic but grounded. Colors: russet, amber, dark brown, restrained crimson. Constraints: no card frame, no text, no logo, no watermark, no extra characters. Original design, not an existing game character.

Composition: vertical portrait, torso and face centered, enough margin for UI card crop. Lighting: forge ember glow on her face, grounded and dignified. Colors: bronze, charcoal, ochre and muted gold. Constraints: no card frame, no text, no logo, no watermark, no extra characters. Original design, not an existing game character.
